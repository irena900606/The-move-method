A structured training method combining weightlifting, strength, gymnastics and conditioning. Train with purpose. Progress with consistency.

WEEKLY PROGRAM

MONDAY

WEIGHTLIFTING

Clean & Jerk · Strength · Conditioning

WARM-UP

5 min easy cardio
Dynamic mobility
Barbell preparation

SKILL / STRENGTH

Clean & Jerk – 5 × 2
Front Squat – 3 × 5

WOD – 12 MIN AMRAP

10 Wall Balls
8 Hang Power Cleans
6 Burpees Over Bar

ACCESSORIES

Face Pulls – 3 × 15
Plank – 3 × 40 sec

TUESDAY

GYMNASTICS

Snatch · Gymnastics · Dumbbells

WARM-UP

5 min cardio
Shoulder and hip mobility

SKILL / STRENGTH

Snatch – 5 × 2
Overhead Squat – 3 × 5

WOD – 4 ROUNDS

10 Dumbbell Push Press
8 Pull-ups
10 Box Jump Overs

ACCESSORIES

Hollow Hold – 3 × 30 sec
Scapular Pull-ups – 3 × 10

WEDNESDAY

ENGINE

Aerobic Capacity · Endurance

MAIN SESSION

20–30 min continuous work
Choose Row, Bike, Ski or Run.

INTENSITY

Moderate and controlled pace. Maintain a steady effort throughout the session.

THURSDAY

GYMNASTICS

Jerk · Pulling Strength · Conditioning

WARM-UP

General warm-up
Shoulder and wrist mobility

SKILL / STRENGTH

Push Jerk – 5 × 3
Strict Pull-ups – 3 × 6

WOD – 10 MIN AMRAP

12 Kettlebell Swings
10 Hand Release Push-ups
8 Box Jumps

ACCESSORIES

Side Plank – 3 × 30 sec each side

FRIDAY

STRENGTH

Squats · Strength · Mixed Conditioning

WARM-UP

5 min easy cardio
Lower-body mobility
Squat preparation

SKILL / STRENGTH

Back Squat – 5 × 5
Bulgarian Split Squat – 3 × 8 each leg

WOD – FOR TIME

400 m Run
20 Deadlifts
15 Burpees

ACCESSORIES

Core Stability – 3 sets

TRAIN SMART. MOVE BETTER. PROGRESS.

Scale movements and loads according to your ability. Prioritize technique, consistency and recovery.
